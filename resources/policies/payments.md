# How payments and earnings work on ModelHub

> **DRAFT for review, 2026-10-02.** Written from how the platform actually behaves today. Items marked **[CONFIRM]** are decisions or numbers we have not finalised; the whole document needs a lawyer's review before it is published, in particular the sections on refunds, disputes, fees and tax. Amounts below are the platform's current settings and can change (see section 13).

This guide explains how money moves on ModelHub: what you pay, what you earn, when you can take it out, and what happens when something goes wrong. It covers both parts of the platform, **3D models** (buying and selling) and **projects** (hiring and being hired). It sits alongside the Terms of Service, the Licence terms for models and the Cancellation policy; where this guide and those disagree, the Terms win.

**All money on ModelHub is in Kenyan shillings (KES), paid and paid out through M-Pesa.** M-Pesa only moves whole shillings, so every price and every agreed amount is a whole number of shillings, and a single payment can be at most **KES 150,000**.

---

## 1. The short version

- **Buying a model:** you pay by M-Pesa, you get your licence and files straight away. The seller's share is held for **7 days** before they can withdraw it. If a file is broken or not as described, you can ask for a refund within that time.
- **Hiring for a project:** after the freelancer accepts your offer you put the agreed amount into **escrow**. It is held safely, and released to the freelancer **as you approve each deliverable**. You never pay more than the agreed amount.
- **Being paid for a project:** your share (the agreed amount less the platform's service fee) reaches your balance as each deliverable is approved, and you can withdraw it at once.
- **Taking money out:** ask for a withdrawal (minimum **KES 500**, fee **KES 30** **[CONFIRM: placeholder until the real M-Pesa tariff is known]**). Our staff approve each one before it is sent to your M-Pesa.
- **If a payment goes wrong,** the money is never lost: it is held and sorted out by our staff, with a reference you can quote (section 11).

## 3. Buying a model

1. Choose a licence on the model's page (**Standard** or, if the seller offers it, **Extended**) and enter your M-Pesa number.
2. We send an M-Pesa prompt to that phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to pay. The prompt is open for **5 minutes**; the page updates by itself.
3. When the payment arrives, your **licence** is issued and you can download the files from **My licences**. You also get a receipt notification.

**Good to know**

- You can hold one licence of each kind for a model. You can upgrade from Standard to Extended, but not the other way round. You cannot buy your own model.
- If the prompt is cancelled, declined (for example, not enough M-Pesa balance) or not answered, **you are not charged** and nothing is issued. You can try again.
- Pressing "pay" twice does not send two prompts.
- Each file you download is recorded. This matters for refunds (section 5).

## 4. Selling models

- When someone buys your model, **your share** (the price less the commission) is credited to your account and **held for 7 days**. During the hold it shows as "in the hold period" on your Earnings page; after it, it becomes **available to withdraw**. **[CONFIRM: the hold length is a platform setting, currently 7 days.]**
- You are told about each sale and what your share is. Your Earnings page lists every sale, whether it is still held, and when it is released.
- If a sale is **refunded** (section 5) your share of it is taken back and you are told.
- You are responsible for your listing being accurate and the files working. Repeated refunds for the same reason may affect your store.

## 5. Refunds for models

Digital files cannot be handed back once you have them, so refunds are limited:

- **When you can ask:** the file is **broken** (does not open, is corrupt, is missing parts) or is **not as described** on the listing. Ask within the **7 days** after your purchase.
- **When we will not refund:** you changed your mind, or you downloaded files that work and match the listing.
- **How to ask:** contact support with your payment reference (shown on your payment page and in your receipt) and say what is wrong. **[CONFIRM: there is no "request a refund" button yet; today this goes through support.]**
- **What happens if we refund:** your licence ends, the files are no longer available to you, and the money is returned to **your M-Pesa**. Our finance team sends it by hand to the number you paid from, so it may take a little while after we confirm. The seller's share and our commission are reversed.
- If the seller has already withdrawn the money, we cannot take it back from their earnings automatically; in that case our team will look at it individually. **[CONFIRM: wording and whether the platform covers it.]**

## 6. Hiring for a project (clients)

1. You make an offer to a freelancer for an agreed amount, with deliverables.
2. **The freelancer accepts.** Nothing is paid yet, and the work has not started.
3. **You fund the job.** On the project's page you enter your M-Pesa number and pay the agreed amount (up to KES 150,000). Work starts once the money arrives. If you do not fund, the freelancer cannot begin.
4. The money is held in **escrow**: it is not the freelancer's yet, and it is not available to us for anything else. It exists only to pay for this project.
5. As the freelancer submits each deliverable you **review it: approve it or ask for changes**. Approving a deliverable releases that deliverable's share of the money to the freelancer.
6. When every deliverable is approved the project is **completed** and all the money has gone to the freelancer (less the service fee, which we keep).

**How the money is split between deliverables.** Each deliverable is worth an equal share of the freelancer's total. Approving one releases its share, so the freelancer is paid steadily as work is accepted. If a deliverable is added later, nothing already released is taken back; the shares of what remains are simply worked out again.

**Example.** Agreed amount KES 1,100 with a 10% fee (KES 100) and three deliverables. The freelancer's total is KES 1,000. Approving the first releases KES 333.33 to them (and KES 33.33 to us as fee); the second KES 333.34 (and 33.34); the third KES 333.33 (and 33.33). Together that is KES 1,000 and KES 100: exactly the agreed amount.

**You pay the agreed amount, no more.** There are no extras for approving work or for the money passing through escrow.

## 7. Being paid for a project

- Your offer shows what you will receive: the agreed amount less the service fee.
- After you accept, the project waits for the client to fund it. **Do not start work until you are told it is funded**; you will be notified, and you cannot submit deliverables before then.
- Each time the client approves a deliverable, your share for it goes straight to your **available balance**. There is **no waiting period** on project earnings, because the client has already approved the work.
- You can follow it on the project's page ("released / in escrow") and on your **Earnings** page under **Job payments**. When your first project has been funded, an **Earnings** link appears in your menu under Projects.
- **Approvals are final.** Once the client has approved a deliverable and you have been paid for it, it is not reversed.

## 9. Cancelling a funded project

Either side can cancel a project (see the Cancellation policy for the process). What happens to the money:

1. **Work already approved is paid.** It was released as it was approved and stays with the freelancer.
2. **Work submitted but not yet reviewed:** the client should approve or reject it first. Approving it releases its share, as usual.
3. **What is left in escrow** is then dealt with according to who cancelled:
   - **The freelancer cancels:** the rest goes back to the client.
   - **The client cancels:** the client has a **7-day review window** in which they can still **pay extra for work that was never approved**, by sending a payment (any amount up to what is left for the freelancer; they may also choose to pay nothing more). The freelancer **accepts** it or **disputes** it. After the window closes without a payment, the rest goes back to the client. **[CONFIRM: window length, currently 7 days.]**
4. While a payment or dispute is open, **the remaining money stays in escrow**; it is not returned to anyone until it is settled.
5. **Disputes.** If the freelancer disputes an amount, our staff review the evidence both sides give and decide a final amount, which is paid to the freelancer out of what is left in escrow; the rest goes back to the client. Staff cannot award more than what is left in escrow for the freelancer. The decision is final on the platform. **[CONFIRM: appeal/escalation path, and the target time for a decision (the dispute page currently says 3-5 business days).]**
6. **Returning money to the client.** When money is due back to a client, our finance team records it and sends it by M-Pesa to the number the client paid from. The client is notified.

If a project was **never funded**, nothing is paid or returned; nobody has paid anything.

## 10. Taking money out (withdrawals)

- Your **available** balance (model sales after the hold, plus project earnings) can be withdrawn to M-Pesa from the **Earnings** page.
- **Minimum KES 500; fee KES 30 [CONFIRM]**; the fee is taken from the amount you withdraw, and the page shows what you will receive before you confirm. Whole shillings only, and at most KES 150,000 can be sent at a time.
- **One withdrawal at a time.** Wait for it to finish, or cancel it while it is still waiting.
- The amount **leaves your balance when you ask**, so it cannot be asked for twice.
- **Our staff approve each withdrawal** before it is sent. **[CONFIRM: how quickly we aim to review them.]** You can cancel while it is waiting for approval. If we turn it down, you are given the reason and the money goes back to your balance.
- When it is approved the money is sent to the M-Pesa number you gave. If the transfer fails, the money returns to your balance and you are told.
- **Check the number carefully.** M-Pesa transfers cannot be recalled. We send to the number you give us.
- In rare cases M-Pesa does not confirm a transfer by itself. Our staff check it against the M-Pesa records and settle it; you will be told the outcome. Please do not ask for the same money again in the meantime.

## 11. When something goes wrong

**Money is never lost on the platform.** A payment that arrives but cannot be matched is held and reviewed by our staff.

| What happened | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You were charged but got no licence / the project is not funded | The payment arrived but could not be matched (for example, a different amount than asked, or the project was cancelled while you were paying) | Nothing is lost. It is held for review and refunded or sorted out by our staff. Contact support with your **payment reference** (shown on your payment page). |
| The M-Pesa prompt never came | Network or phone problem; nothing was charged | Check your number and try again. |
| Prompt cancelled or declined | Nothing was charged | Try again when you are ready. |
| Withdrawal shows "being sent" for a long time | M-Pesa has not confirmed it yet | Wait. Our staff are alerted and will check. Do not ask again for the same money. |
| Withdrawal turned down | The reason is shown | Fix what it says (for example the number) and ask again. |
| A dispute about project money | See section 9 | Give clear details and evidence on the dispute form. |

Always quote your **payment reference** or **withdrawal reference** when you contact support; both are shown on your pages and in your notifications.
